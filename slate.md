AI Configuration Review

Assess the security configuration of your enterprise AI environment.

The rapid adoption of generative AI has introduced a new layer of technology risk for organizations. Enterprise AI platforms can provide access to sensitive information, powerful capabilities, and broad user populations, making the underlying configuration an important component of an organization’s security and data protection posture. Configuration weaknesses can create unnecessary exposure even where broader cybersecurity controls are mature.

ACA’s AI Configuration Review provides a focused assessment of a single AI platform instance or tenant, evaluating the configuration controls that govern user access, permissions, platform security, and data protection. The review identifies areas where configurations may not align with security requirements and provides practical recommendations for remediation.

What to Expect

ACA begins by reviewing the configuration of the selected AI environment against security and data protection considerations relevant to the platform. The assessment covers:

- Platform Configuration: Review of security-relevant settings and controls configured within the AI platform, identifying settings that may introduce unnecessary risk or weaken the environment’s security posture.

- User Access & Permissions: Assessment of how users and administrators are granted access, with a focus on identifying excessive, inappropriate, or otherwise unnecessary permissions.

- Data Protection: Evaluation of configuration settings relevant to how organizational information is protected within the AI environment, including controls that may affect exposure of sensitive data.

- Security Gaps: Identification and documentation of configuration weaknesses, with findings prioritized to provide context around their potential security and data protection implications.

- Recommendations: Development of actionable recommendations addressing identified configuration gaps and providing a practical path toward remediation.

The engagement covers one instance or tenant of Anthropic Claude, OpenAI ChatGPT, or Microsoft Copilot. Following the assessment, ACA provides a formal report detailing the findings and recommendations and conducts a one-hour virtual readout with the client. Up to two report iterations are included following the readout to incorporate feedback or clarify findings.
